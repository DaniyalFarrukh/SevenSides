# Menu Page Override
- **Layout**: Sticky category tabs on mobile (bottom or top) and desktop (sidebar or top).
- **Interactions**: Fast scroll-spy for category sections.
- **Visuals**: Emphasize food imagery with full-bleed cards or large thumbnails.
- **Components**: Item modal with add-ons and variants. "Add to Cart" sticky at bottom of modal. Out-of-stock badges.
- **Density**: Medium density. Allow quick scanning of prices and item names.
